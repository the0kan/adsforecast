# AdsForecast brand assets

## Identity idea

The AdsForecast mark combines ascending performance bars with a forward forecast trajectory. The mint origin point represents verified input data; the violet-to-coral path represents a recommendation moving from evidence to action.

## Primary assets

- `assets/logo-mark.svg` — primary product and navigation mark
- `assets/logo-horizontal.svg` — full horizontal lockup
- `assets/favicon.svg` — browser favicon
- `assets/apple-touch-icon.png` — iOS home-screen icon
- `assets/icon-192.png` and `assets/icon-512.png` — installable web-app icons
- `assets/adsforecast-social-card.png` — 1200 × 630 social sharing image

## Usage

- Keep clear space around the symbol equal to at least one quarter of its width.
- Do not place the mark below 16 × 16 pixels.
- Do not recolor individual bars or redraw the forecast path.
- Prefer the supplied dark tile on both dark and light surfaces so the identity remains consistent.
- Keep the wordmark horizontal and use the product typeface, Manrope, when recreating text in UI.

## Core colors

- Ink: `#0B0912`
- Violet: `#8B7CF6`
- Magenta: `#F05CA8`
- Coral: `#FF755F`
- Verified signal: `#55DFC0`
- Primary text: `#F7F5FA`
