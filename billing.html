Billing integrity

Verified status only

A plan is marked active only after a signed Stripe event confirms the subscription. Plan selection and checkout drafts are kept separate from paid access.

Billing questions

Before you choose

What changes on annual billing?

You pay once for the year and receive the lower effective monthly price shown on each plan.

Will saving a plan charge me?

No. Saving a selection creates a server-side checkout draft. A charge can only occur inside secure Stripe checkout when it is configured and you confirm payment there.

How is plan activation verified?

Subscription state changes only after AdsForecast verifies a signed Stripe webhook event.

Is my Meta connection affected?

No. Selecting or changing a billing plan does not disconnect Meta or alter campaign data.
