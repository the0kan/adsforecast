Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Overview

AdsForecast is an analytics workspace for commerce brands. It helps connect advertising and store data so teams can understand spend, revenue, profitability, and campaign performance in one place.

This policy explains the information AdsForecast may process while operating the product. It is written for an early-stage product and may be updated as functionality evolves.

Information we may process

  • Account information. Name, email address, workspace membership, and authentication-related identifiers.
  • Meta Ads data. When you connect Meta, we may process ad account identifiers, campaign metrics, spend, purchase value, and performance fields available through your permissions.
  • Commerce data. Future WooCommerce or Shopify connections may include orders, product metadata, revenue, fees, returns, and margin-related information.
  • Usage and diagnostics. Technical logs and product interaction data needed to secure, debug, and improve the service.

How information is used

We use information to provide dashboard reporting, operate integrations, generate recommendations, maintain security, improve product quality, and communicate with users when needed.

Sharing and subprocessors

We do not sell personal data. We may share information with infrastructure providers and connected platforms where necessary to host, secure, and operate AdsForecast. Third-party platforms process data according to their own terms and policies.

Your choices

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your information subject to legal, security, and operational requirements. For privacy requests, email privacy@adsforecast.com.
