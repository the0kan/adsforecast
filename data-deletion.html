Data Deletion Request

Last updated: April 2026

Request deletion of your data

If you use AdsForecast and want your data deleted, you can submit a request by contacting our privacy team. Deletion may include account data, workspace metadata, connected integration records, and analytics records used for AdsForecast reporting.

How to request deletion

Email your request to privacy@adsforecast.com from the account email associated with your workspace. Include enough detail for us to identify your account and connected workspace.

We review and process valid requests within a reasonable time, subject to legal, security, and operational requirements.

Disconnecting integrations

You can also disconnect AdsForecast from third-party platforms through those platforms’ own app or integration settings where available.
